# Welcome to Chasing Cloud Careers

We're excited to launch **Chasing Cloud Careers LLC**, a platform dedicated to providing free, accessible tech education for everyone.

## What We Do

Chasing Cloud Careers LLC provides:

- **Public speaking** on technical education
- **Mentorship** for aspiring tech professionals
- **Tech bootcamp cohorts** for hands-on learning
- **Free self-service training materials** and learning paths

## Our Focus Areas

We're building learning paths covering:

- **Cloud Computing** — AWS, Azure, GCP fundamentals and beyond
- **AI & Machine Learning** — From concepts to implementation
- **DevOps & Infrastructure** — CI/CD, containers, infrastructure as code

## Get Started

Head over to our [Learning Paths](/learn/) to start your journey, or follow us on [GitHub](https://github.com/chasingcloudcareersllc) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/chasing-cloud-careers) to stay updated.

Your cloud career starts here.
